Understanding Male Infertility
Causes, Diagnosis & Solutions

Key Takeaway

Male Infertility accounts for a significant portion of fertility struggles. It involves any health issue in a man that lowers the chances of his female partner getting pregnant.

Causes

Why Does It Happen?

Male infertility can be caused by various medical, environmental, and lifestyle factors that affect the production, quality, or transport of sperm.

  • Varicoceles (swollen veins in the scrotum).
  • Infections or hormonal imbalances.
  • Blockages in the reproductive tract.
  • Exposure to toxins, smoking, and heavy alcohol use.
Diagnosis

Testing and Evaluation

A proper diagnosis begins with a thorough medical history and a comprehensive semen analysis to evaluate sperm count, motility, and morphology.

  • Advanced Semen Analysis.
  • Scrotal Ultrasounds.
  • Hormone testing (Testosterone, FSH, etc.).
  • Genetic testing for severe cases.
