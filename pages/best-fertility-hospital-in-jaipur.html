Core Pillars that Set Our Hospital Apart

Achieving high clinical success rates requires an absolute commitment to quality across every phase of treatment. At Kulki IVF, our infrastructure is meticulously designed to support embryonic development under optimal conditions:

  • Medical-Grade Laboratory Cleanrooms: Equipped with advanced air filtration and continuous environmental monitoring to protect delicate cells from external air impurities.
  • Advanced Micromanipulation and Laser Systems: Utilizing ultra-precise laser systems for assisted embryo hatching and high-magnification optics for ICSI sperm selection.
  • Extended Day-5 Blastocyst Culture: Culturing embryos systematically to advanced developmental stages, delivering higher natural implantation rates.
  • Rapid Cryopreservation Solutions: Providing secure vitrification technology to preserve eggs, sperm, and embryos safely for future cycles.
