Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)
A Gentle Step Towards Conception

Key Takeaway

Intrauterine Insemination (IUI) is a simple, non-invasive fertility treatment where specially washed sperm is placed directly into the uterus during ovulation to facilitate fertilization.

Overview

What is IUI?

IUI is a fertility treatment that involves placing specially washed sperm directly into the uterus during the ovulation period. It is less invasive than IVF and often a great first step.

  • Less invasive and more affordable.
  • Great for mild male factor infertility.
  • Helps with cervical mucus issues.
  • Can be done with or without fertility medications.
Procedure

How It Works

The procedure is quick and practically painless, similar to a routine pelvic exam. We carefully time the insemination with your natural or stimulated ovulation.

  • Follicle Tracking via Ultrasound.
  • Sperm Washing and Preparation.
  • Painless Insemination Process.
  • Short Recovery and Follow-up.
