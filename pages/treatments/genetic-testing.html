Preimplantation Genetic Testing (PGT)
Ensuring the Health of Your Future Baby

Key Takeaway

Preimplantation Genetic Testing (PGT) is performed during IVF to screen embryos for chromosomal abnormalities or specific genetic diseases before they are transferred to the uterus.

Overview

What is PGT?

PGT involves testing embryos created through IVF for genetic abnormalities before they are transferred to the uterus. This helps select the healthiest embryos, increasing the chance of a successful, healthy pregnancy.

  • Reduces the risk of miscarriage.
  • Identifies chromosomal abnormalities (PGT-A).
  • Screens for single-gene disorders (PGT-M).
  • Increases IVF success rates per transfer.
The Process

Precision Diagnostics

A few cells are safely biopsied from a blastocyst-stage embryo by our skilled embryologists. The embryo is then frozen while the cells are analyzed in our genetics lab.

  • Safe embryo biopsy techniques.
  • Advanced Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS).
  • Expert genetic counseling available.
  • Clear, actionable results for transfer planning.
