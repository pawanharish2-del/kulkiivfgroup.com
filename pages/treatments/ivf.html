In Vitro Fertilization (IVF)
Advanced Care for Your Path to Parenthood

Key Takeaway

In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) is an advanced reproductive technique where eggs are fertilized by sperm outside the body. It is highly effective for patients with blocked fallopian tubes, severe male infertility, or unexplained infertility.

Overview

What is IVF?

In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) is one of the most advanced and successful fertility treatments available today. It involves retrieving mature eggs from the ovaries and fertilizing them with sperm in our state-of-the-art laboratory.

  • Highly effective for blocked fallopian tubes.
  • Ideal for severe male factor infertility.
  • Addresses unexplained infertility and endometriosis.
  • Utilizes state-of-the-art laboratory environments.
The Process

The IVF Journey

Our dedicated team guides you through every step of the IVF journey, ensuring you feel supported and informed.

  • Ovarian Stimulation & Monitoring.
  • Minimally Invasive Egg Retrieval.
  • Expert Fertilization & Embryo Culture.
  • Precise Embryo Transfer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Most patients experience only mild discomfort. The egg retrieval is done under light anesthesia, so you will not feel pain during the procedure.

A typical IVF cycle takes about 3 to 4 weeks from the start of medication to the embryo transfer.

Success rates depend on various factors like age and the cause of infertility, but Kulki IVF maintains some of the highest success rates in the region due to our advanced laboratory.
