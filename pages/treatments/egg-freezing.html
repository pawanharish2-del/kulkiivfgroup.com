Egg Freezing (Oocyte Cryopreservation)
Preserving Your Future Fertility

Key Takeaway

Egg Freezing allows women to preserve their fertility by extracting, freezing, and storing their eggs. This empowers women to delay motherhood for medical or personal reasons without compromising egg quality.

Overview

Why Freeze Your Eggs?

Egg freezing allows women to pause their biological clock by preserving their eggs when they are at their healthiest. This provides peace of mind for future family planning.

  • Career or educational focus.
  • Medical reasons (such as chemotherapy).
  • Delaying childbirth for personal reasons.
  • Preserves egg quality at your current age.
Technology

Advanced Vitrification

We use a rapid-freezing technique called vitrification, which prevents ice crystal formation and drastically improves the survival rate of the eggs when thawed.

  • Ultra-rapid cooling process.
  • High post-thaw survival rates.
  • Safe and secure long-term storage.
  • Maintains cellular integrity.

Frequently Asked Questions

The optimal age for egg freezing is generally between the late 20s and early 30s when egg quality and quantity are at their peak.

Using advanced vitrification, eggs can remain safely frozen indefinitely without losing their viability.
