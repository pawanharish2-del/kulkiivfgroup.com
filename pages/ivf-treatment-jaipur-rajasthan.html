What is IVF Treatment?

In Vitro Fertilization, popularly known as IVF, is one of the most effective forms of Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART). IVF treatment involves stimulating a woman's ovaries to produce multiple eggs, retrieving those eggs, fertilizing them with sperm in a controlled laboratory environment, and then transferring the resulting embryo(s) into the uterus.

IVF treatment in Rajasthan has grown tremendously over the past decade, with more and more couples from cities like Jaipur, Ajmer, Dausa, Sikar, and Alwar seeking this life-changing procedure. The process is medically supervised at every step, ensuring the highest possible safety standards for both mother and child.

Why is IVF Needed? Common Causes of Infertility

Infertility affects approximately 10–15% of couples in India. IVF treatment in Jaipur is recommended for couples dealing with:

  • Blocked or damaged fallopian tubes – preventing natural fertilization
  • Male factor infertility – low sperm count, poor motility, or abnormal morphology
  • Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) – affecting ovulation regularity
  • Endometriosis – a condition where uterine tissue grows outside the uterus
  • Unexplained infertility – where standard tests reveal no specific cause
  • Advanced maternal age – declining egg quality and quantity after 35
  • Recurrent miscarriages – requiring genetic screening before implantation

Step-by-Step IVF Treatment Process at Kulki IVF, Jaipur

Understanding the IVF treatment process in Jaipur helps reduce anxiety and sets realistic expectations. Here is how our team at Kulki IVF guides you through each phase:

Step 1: Initial Consultation & Fertility Assessment

Your journey at Kulki IVF Jaipur begins with a thorough consultation. Our experienced fertility specialists — Dr. Asha Sushawat and Dr. Aarti Soni — conduct a detailed medical history review, hormonal blood tests, ultrasound scans, and semen analysis to create a personalized treatment roadmap.

Step 2: Ovarian Stimulation

The female partner receives hormonal injections over 10–14 days to stimulate the ovaries into producing multiple mature eggs. This step is carefully monitored through serial ultrasounds and blood tests to prevent ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome (OHSS).

Step 3: Egg Retrieval (Oocyte Pick-Up)

Under mild sedation, mature eggs are retrieved from the ovaries using a fine needle guided by ultrasound. The procedure takes approximately 20–30 minutes and is minimally invasive.

Step 4: Fertilization in the Laboratory

The retrieved eggs are fertilized with the partner's or donor's sperm in our state-of-the-art IVF laboratory. At Kulki IVF Fertility Centre, Jaipur, our expert embryologist Dr. Ssuniti monitors embryo development around the clock in advanced incubators that replicate natural uterine conditions.

Step 5: Embryo Culture & Development

Fertilized eggs are cultured for 3 to 5 days until they reach the blastocyst stage. Our laboratory uses cutting-edge time-lapse embryo monitoring to select only the highest-quality embryos for transfer.

Step 6: Embryo Transfer

The selected embryo(s) are gently placed into the uterus using a fine catheter. This is a painless, non-surgical procedure that takes only a few minutes. Remaining viable embryos can be cryopreserved (frozen) for future cycles.

Step 7: Pregnancy Test

Approximately 12–14 days after embryo transfer, a blood beta-hCG test confirms whether the IVF cycle has resulted in a successful pregnancy. Our team provides continuous emotional and medical support throughout the two-week wait.
